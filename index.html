No future-price leakage

A trained predictor requires ≥80 independent, fully matured 90-day outcomes including at least 20 wins and 20 losses across 3+ sectors. Historic chart tests alone do not establish news-signal predictiveness.

Backtesting limitations

The price cache uses adjusted daily OHLCV from an unofficial free source. Same-bar stop/target conflicts assume the stop triggers first. Costs and gap risk are approximations. Today's selected tickers introduce survivorship bias, and analysing many parameter combinations creates overfitting risk. Never treat an in-sample winning strategy as a validated live edge.
